Field Manual  ·  DiplomacySim Briefing Desk

DiplomacySim puts you inside tense policy rooms where information is incomplete and every option has a price. You may be a foreign minister, an envoy, a central banker, or a crisis adviser. Your job is simple to state and difficult to execute: choose the most defensible course before the clock closes.

"Good strategy is not about finding a perfect move. It is about choosing the least fragile one under pressure."

Each scenario gives you a concise briefing, a role, and four possible directives. Some options protect credibility. Some buy time. Some look decisive but create new damage. The point values stay hidden until after you commit.

Your score reflects both judgment and speed. After each decision, the consequence report reveals every option so you can learn from the trade-offs instead of guessing blindly next time.
