After you enter a theatre, read the briefing carefully and choose one directive. Your choice is final once submitted.

75–100Optimal
Strong decision. Your directive meets the core objective while limiting diplomatic, economic, or humanitarian damage.
40–74Adequate
Defensible, but costly. Your choice solves part of the problem while leaving a serious risk unresolved.
10–39Weak
Weak judgment. The move worsens leverage, alienates partners, or gives opposing actors more room.
0–9Damaging
Counterproductive. The decision creates avoidable damage and leaves the original crisis harder to solve.
