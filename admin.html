Writing Guidelines
1
Situation briefs should establish the actors, stakes, constraints, and immediate trigger in 2-4 focused sentences.
2
The decision prompt should give the player a role and one clear problem to solve.
3
Options A-D should represent genuinely different strategies, not four versions of the same move.
4
Consequences should explain cause and effect. Avoid vague praise or punishment.
5
Point values stay hidden until submission. Assign them for strategic quality, not narrative preference.
